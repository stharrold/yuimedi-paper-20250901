Hi Natalia,

Thank you, this looks great. The visual abstract captures the paper accurately, and I am
happy with the design, the structure, and the flow.

I am the sole author, so the feedback below is already consolidated and final. I have one
correction, and both parts of it sit in the same text block, at the bottom of the first
column under "The triple threat".

Current text:

  "This study calls for the Human-in-the-Loop Knowledge Governance (HITL-KG) to shift
   knowledge from human memory to durable artifacts"

Requested text:

  "This viewpoint calls for the Human-in-the-Loop Knowledge Governance (HITL-KG) framework
   to shift knowledge from human memory to durable artifacts"

Two reasons, in order of importance:

1. "This study" to "This viewpoint". The article is a Viewpoint and reports no primary
   data. The manuscript reserves the word "study" for the empirical companion paper, and
   states that "empirical validation is deferred to a companion study." "Viewpoint" avoids
   setting an expectation of data that this article does not contain, which matters most
   for the readers who will meet the paper through the visual abstract rather than the
   full text.

2. Adding "framework" after (HITL-KG). Without it, the sentence has no noun for "to shift"
   to attach to. "The HITL-KG framework" is the phrasing used in the manuscript, and it
   also matches the heading you already use on the middle panel.

One optional item, entirely at your discretion: the em dash in "institutional
amnesia"-the loss of tacit knowledge. The manuscript uses no em dashes, so a colon would
match its style. Please treat that as a preference rather than a request, and skip it if it
complicates the layout.

Everything else I have checked against the accepted manuscript and confirmed as accurate:
the triple threat wording, the 3 pillars, the 6 steps of the validated query cycle and
their order, the summary line, the byline, the citation, the DOI, and the URL. No other
changes.

Thank you again for putting this together.

Best,
Samuel
