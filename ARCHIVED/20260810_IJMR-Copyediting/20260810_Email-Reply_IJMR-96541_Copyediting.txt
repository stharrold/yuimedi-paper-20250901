Dear Copyeditor,

I have completed my review of the copyedited manuscript, addressed all queries
in the system, and clicked Approve and Submit. Thank you for the careful edit;
several of your catches were substantive.

A few notes on how I handled specific queries:

Three-pillar structure (Table 1 discussion). You were right that the term had
not been introduced in the body. I added a defining sentence at first use: "The
framework's 3 pillars name the organizational capabilities at stake in the
'triple threat': analytics maturity, workforce agility, and technical
enablement."

AI queries. Also correct; the body never established that the queries are
AI-generated. I revised the sentence so it introduces this rather than assuming
it, naming the conversational AI system at first mention.

26 organizations. Worldwide. Both figures are global counts from the HIMSS
Analytics AMAM global report. They were announced at the HIMSS24 Asia-Pacific
conference in October 2024 but are not regional, and they are the same cohort
described earlier in the article as "39 organizations globally" (26 at stage 6
plus 13 at stage 7). I have added "worldwide" to remove the ambiguity.

Turnover cost sentence. Confirmed, with one small change: I retained the word
"total" ("incurring a total cost of up to US $8.97 million"). The preceding
clause gives a per-employee range, and "total" is what distinguishes the
aggregate figure from another per-person one.

Data availability statement. I adopted template 4, "Data sharing is not
applicable to this article as no data sets were generated or analyzed during
this study," which fits a Viewpoint with no primary data collection. I retained
two following sentences: one noting that all cited figures come from published
literature, and one disclosing the code deposit, since the knowledge base states
that code generated during the study should also be included. I left the Zenodo
identifier as an inline DOI rather than adding it to the reference list, as the
instruction to cite a persistent identifier in the reference list appears under
template 1, where the deposit is the study's dataset. Please let me know if you
would prefer it as a numbered reference.

Figures. Both have been regenerated from source and re-uploaded, with all text
set to sentence case, "Healthcare" revised to "Health care," and Figure 2's
"THREE PILLARS" revised to "3 pillars." On the plus signs in Figure 1, I
replaced them with the word "and" rather than closing up the spaces: "NLP
engine+SQL generation" can be misread as "NLP (engine+SQL) generation," since a
plus sign binds tightly to the tokens on either side. This satisfies the request
while removing the ambiguity, but I am happy to supply the closed-up form if you
prefer it.

References 28 and 61. Yes to both URLs, and thank you for querying them; my
submitted entries were wrong. Two corrections to the entries as they stood:

Reference 28: the published title reads "design workspaces," not "design
spaces," and the second author is Ge X rather than GE X (the source PDF prints
author names in full capitals). Your page range and author list were otherwise
correct.

Reference 61: I have given the full title, "The code modernization playbook:
transforming legacy systems with AI," and added the publication year 2025, so
the entry matches the other web references that carry a year.

I should also thank you for two corrections you made without querying: the 2026
edition of the NSI retention report, and the 2025 date on the Bravo Rocca
thesis. Both were errors in my bibliography, and I have corrected them at
source.

Please let me know if anything further is needed before the proofs.

Best regards,
