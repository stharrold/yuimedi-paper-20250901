## Notes on the drafting (not sent)

**Opens by quoting her own invitation.** This is a chase on 3 late items, and the single
best way to keep it collegial is to make clear she asked for it.

**Each item states an observation, not an accusation.** "I have not been able to find it"
and "I am still not seeing" describe what is missing from the author's side and leave room
for the answer to be "it ran, here it is." That matters most on the tweet, where the
evidence (JMIR's own Tweetations tab) is strong but may simply not capture promoted posts.

**PubMed carries the one piece of active argument**, because her "cache or slight delay"
explanation has weakened: PMC and PubMed have now been out of step for a week, and a cache
would not hold one NCBI database and not its sibling that long. Stated as a fact about the
2 records rather than as a rebuttal.

**Ends with "no urgency."** True, and it keeps the message from reading as escalation. The
items are late, not damaging.

### Deliberately not included

- **Amounts.** Naming the $99 and $250 would turn a status chase into a billing dispute.
  Nothing so far suggests bad faith, only slippage.
- **The methodology** (Playwright, the Snowdon widget baseline, the 2-observation
  comparison). Correct, and it is why the claims are trustworthy, but it would read as
  building a case. The evidence lives in
  `20260907_Purchased-Items-Delivery-Status.md` if it is ever needed.
- **Any deadline of our own.** She has been reliable and responsive throughout; imposing
  one now would be out of keeping with the thread.

### If she does not respond within a week
