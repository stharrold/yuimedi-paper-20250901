## Requested change

**Location:** column 1 ("The triple threat"), third and lowest text block, next to the
circular AI icon.

**Current text:**

> This study calls for the Human-in-the-Loop Knowledge Governance (HITL-KG) to shift
> knowledge from human memory to durable artifacts

**Requested text:**

> This **viewpoint** calls for the Human-in-the-Loop Knowledge Governance (HITL-KG)
> **framework** to shift knowledge from human memory to durable artifacts

Two words change: "study" becomes "viewpoint", and "framework" is added after the acronym.

### Reason 1: "This study" to "This viewpoint"

The article is a Viewpoint and reports no primary data. The manuscript reserves the word
"study" for the empirical companion paper, stating that "empirical validation is deferred to
a companion study."

Readers who meet the paper through the visual abstract rather than the full text are
exactly the ones who would be misled, since "this study" sets an expectation of data the
article does not contain.

### Reason 2: adding "framework" after (HITL-KG)

Without it, the sentence has no noun for the infinitive "to shift" to attach to.

"The HITL-KG framework" is the phrasing used throughout the manuscript, and it also matches
the heading already used on the middle panel of this visual abstract.
